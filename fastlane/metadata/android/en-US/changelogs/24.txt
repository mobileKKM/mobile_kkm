First beta of the rebuilt app.

- Buy period tickets in the app and pay with BLIK, card, Google Pay or bank transfer
- Tickets are synced to your phone for offline use; control codes work online only
- Account, mKKM card set-up and Karta Krakowska status
- Map preview: vehicles and departures are not available yet
- The 5+1 subscription is not available yet

This is a beta; please report problems on GitHub.
